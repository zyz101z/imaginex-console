How to play

🖱️ Shoot: press anywhere and drag. Default is slingshot: drag away from where you want the ball to go. Prefer pushing toward the target? Flip it with the 🎯 Aim button. Longer drag = harder hit; drag back to the start to cancel.

⌨️ Keyboard: ← → to aim (hold Shift for fine steps), hold SPACE to charge (fills up and holds), release to hit. R faces the cup. Q/E orbit.

📷 Camera: right-drag (or two fingers) to look around, wheel to zoom. 🎯 button re-faces the cup.

⛳ Rules: water and the void cost a stroke. 8 strokes max per hole. Under par = more points, more coins, more stars.

📈 Progression: every point is XP. Levels pay coins and mulligans (↩ undoes your last shot in Quick Round and Career). Three quests a day and 20 achievements in your Profile. Clear a kingdom's finale in Career for a crown; 27 stars turns it gold.

🎡 Obstacles: bumpers add speed, windmills and moving walls need timing, green arrows boost, blue rings teleport to the pink ring, cannons fire you to the yellow target, sand and syrup are slow, ice is slick, and the guardians just get in the way.
